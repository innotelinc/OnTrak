The family re-baseline. M0–M6 and every shipped part of M7 — assist, analytics,
backup/DR, SOC 2 controls, concurrent-write hardening and the recorded load
rehearsal — are now **2.0.0**. The two scale fixes the rehearsal named (a paged
worklist, and the reference mark held in a row) are **2.1**, followed by HA (2.2),
scheduled exports (2.3), config-as-code (2.4), evidence-grade assurance (3.0) and
the policy-bound desk (4.0). No code changed in this release. See
[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
