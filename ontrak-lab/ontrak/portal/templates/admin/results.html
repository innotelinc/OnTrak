How a grade is made

final = machine × (1 − ticket_weight) + ticket × ticket_weight, where the ticket weight comes from the scenario's ticket.form.weight. A scenario without a ticket form is graded on machine state alone, and a scenario where the machine could not be graded at all stays at zero however good the write-up is.
