Where accounts come from

An account created here signs in with its own password. An account that arrives through Authentik — when SSO is on — is created the first time its owner signs in, keyed on their email address, and carries no password: the local password form can never open it.

Membership of the instructor group in Authentik is what makes an SSO account an instructor, and it is re-read on every sign-in — changing a role below only sticks until that person signs in again. For a local account the role set here is the role.

To take an SSO account off the board, disable it: it stays disabled even when its owner signs in through SSO. Create, rename and offboard SSO people in Authentik.
