The switch is stored in this range's database, so it survives a restart. To provision Authentik itself, use scripts/authentik-setup.py and set the four ONTRAK_PORTAL__OIDC_* values in .env, then switch it on here.

How SSO accounts behave

An account that arrives through Authentik is keyed on its email address, and its role follows the instructor group on every sign-in. It carries no password, so the local password form can never open it — disabling it here still keeps its owner out, even through SSO, and the person can sign back in after a local delete.
